# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior include:
- Being respectful and friendly.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Helping other community members.
- Respecting different opinions.

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment or discrimination.
- Insulting or offensive comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions related to the project.

## Enforcement
Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. They will review and respond appropriately.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Project maintainers may:
1. Issue a warning.
2. Temporarily suspend participation.
3. Permanently remove participation privileges.
